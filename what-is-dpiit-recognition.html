What is DPIIT Startup India Recognition?

DPIIT (Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade) Recognition is a free government recognition for eligible Indian startups under the Startup India initiative. Unlocks tax exemptions, angel tax relief, IPR rebates, and government procurement preferences.

Eligibility

Who qualifies?

  • Indian entity: Pvt Ltd, LLP, Partnership
  • Incorporated < 10 years from formation date
  • Turnover < ₹100 crore in any FY since incorporation
  • Working towards innovation, development, improvement of products/services/processes
  • Or having a scalable business model with high potential for employment/wealth

Benefits

Major benefits.

  • Section 80-IAC: 100% income tax exemption for 3 of first 10 FYs (with IMB approval)
  • Angel tax exemption — Section 56(2)(viib) — for share premium above FMV
  • 80% rebate on patent filing, 50% on trademark
  • Self-certification under 9 labour and environmental laws
  • Public procurement: exemption from prior experience + turnover criteria
  • Eligible for Startup India Seed Fund (up to ₹20L grant + ₹50L convertible debenture)
  • Easier winding-up under Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code

How to apply

Application process.

  • Visit startupindia.gov.in
  • Sign up with founder email
  • Complete startup profile
  • Upload Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, business brief (250 words)
  • Submit for review
  • Receive DPIIT certificate via email — typically 2-7 days
