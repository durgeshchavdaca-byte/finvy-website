/ The default structure

WOS as Pvt Ltd.

For most foreign companies entering India, the right structure is a Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd) with 100% foreign shareholding (Wholly-Owned Subsidiary, 'WOS'). Benefits:

  • FDI permitted via automatic route in most sectors (no prior approval needed).
  • Separate legal entity — limited liability.
  • Easy to operate commercially (sign contracts, hire employees, invoice customers).
  • Standard tax rates (25% / 22% under Section 115BAA).
  • Easy to repatriate profits via dividend (subject to Section 195 TDS + DTAA).

Alternatives (less common): Branch Office, Liaison Office, Project Office, LLP (foreign investment restricted). See our separate guide on Branch vs Liaison vs WOS.

/ Pre-incorporation requirements

What foreign parent must have.

  • Minimum 2 shareholders: foreign parent company + 1 nominee (nominal 1-share holder; can be another entity or individual).
  • Minimum 2 directors: at least ONE must be an Indian resident (physically present in India >120 days in the FY). BQP arranges resident director if parent has no India-resident person.
  • Foreign director's documents: passport + address proof (utility bill/bank statement, apostilled or Hague Convention authenticated).
  • Foreign parent documents: Certificate of Incorporation + Board resolution authorising India subsidiary + apostille/authentication.
  • Registered office in India: can be virtual office / co-working / BQP office address initially; must be updated to actual within 30 days.
  • FDI sector check: verify the proposed business falls under automatic route (default for most IT/SaaS/trading/manufacturing). Prohibited sectors: lottery, chit funds, real estate dealing, atomic energy. Press Note 3 (2020) (eased for non-controlling stakes within 10% by Press Note 2 (2026)): any investment from countries sharing land border with India (China, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan, Myanmar, Afghanistan) requires prior government approval.

/ Incorporation workflow

30-45 day execution.

  1. DSC (Digital Signature Certificate) for all proposed directors — 2-3 days; foreign directors can obtain via online KYC + apostilled passport.
  2. DIN (Director Identification Number) applied through SPICe+ Part B — approved along with incorporation.
  3. Name reservation via SPICe+ Part A — propose 2 names; MCA approves within 1-2 days.
  4. SPICe+ Part B incorporation: MOA, AOA, declarations, consent forms, apostilled documents. Filing fee + stamp duty vary by state and authorised capital.
  5. Certificate of Incorporation + PAN + TAN: issued together by MCA / Income Tax Department. 5-10 working days.
  6. Bank account: open current account with HDFC / ICICI / Kotak / Axis. Resident director typically signs; foreign director KYC can be remote. 7-14 days.
  7. Share capital remittance: foreign parent wires subscription money to India bank account. AD bank issues FIRC (Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate).
  8. FC-GPR filing within 30 days of receiving remittance — mandatory FEMA compliance. Non-filing attracts compounding penalty.
  9. Post-incorporation: GST registration (if applicable), Professional Tax (state-specific), Shops & Establishment, PF & ESI (once headcount triggers).

/ Ongoing compliance (year 1 onwards)

The India compliance calendar.

  • Statutory audit: mandatory for all Pvt Ltd regardless of size. Signed by an Indian CA. Done remotely for foreign-owned companies — BQP delivers audit from India.
  • Income tax return: Form ITR-6 by 31 October (if TP applicable: 30 November).
  • Tax audit: if turnover > INR 1 crore.
  • Transfer pricing audit (Form 3CEB [Form 48 from tax year 2026-27]): mandatory for any transaction with foreign parent (always > INR 1 crore threshold triggered immediately). Form 3CEB by 31 October.
  • ROC annual filings: AOC-4 (financials), MGT-7 (annual return).
  • FLA Return (RBI): annual filing of foreign liabilities & assets — our FLA guide.
  • GST monthly / quarterly returns: GSTR-1 + GSTR-3B.
  • TDS quarterly returns: Form 26Q / 24Q.
  • Board meetings: minimum 4 per year (quarterly).
  • AGM: within 6 months of FY end (30 September).

BQP runs this entire calendar remotely. Foreign parent gets quarterly MIS + annual audited financials.

/ What foreign clients often get wrong

Avoidable mistakes.

  • Press Note 3 trap: investor based in China / Hong Kong routing via Singapore holdco. Beneficial ownership check applies — prior government approval needed. Must disclose BO.
  • FDI sector cap violation: assuming 100% FDI permitted when sector cap is lower (e.g., multi-brand retail trading 51%, print media 26%, defence 74%). Pre-check.
  • No resident director: trying to incorporate with only foreign directors. Section 149(3) Companies Act requires at least 1 director to be resident in India (>120 days in FY).
  • FC-GPR missed deadline: 30-day window from receipt of remittance. Late filing requires compounding under FEMA Section 13 — INR 10,000-50,000 per instance.
  • Apostille vs attestation confusion: Hague Convention member countries (USA, UK, EU, Singapore, UAE, etc.) use apostille. Non-Hague countries need embassy attestation + MEA attestation chain. Different docs; different timelines.
  • Missing Form 3CEB: thinking TP audit doesn't apply because 'it's just a subsidiary of my own company'. Any transaction with foreign parent is Associated Enterprise — Form 3CEB mandatory. See our TP guide.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Can a US / UK / Singapore company own 100% of an Indian subsidiary?
Yes - via FDI automatic route in most sectors. 100% foreign ownership of a Pvt Ltd WOS is standard. Exceptions: prohibited sectors (lottery, chit funds, real estate dealing, atomic energy) and sector-cap limited sectors (multi-brand retail, print media, defence). Press Note 3 restriction applies for countries sharing land border with India (China, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan, Myanmar, Afghanistan) requiring prior government approval.
Do I need to visit India to incorporate my Indian subsidiary?
No. Full remote execution. Foreign directors can obtain DSC + DIN remotely via online KYC with apostilled passport. Documents (MOA/AOA/consents) signed digitally. Bank account can be opened with foreign director KYC (video or in-person at bank's foreign branch). The foreign parent does not need to visit India.
Why is a resident director mandatory in India?
Section 149(3) Companies Act 2013: every Indian company must have at least one director who has stayed in India for 182 days in the preceding FY (effectively a resident). This is to ensure accountable local presence. BQP arranges resident director nominee if the parent has no India-resident person - typical fee USD 1,500-3,000/year scope-based.
How long does Indian WOS incorporation take?
30-45 days end-to-end: DSC (3 days) + name reservation (2 days) + SPICe+ incorporation (10 days) + bank account (14 days) + FC-GPR filing after capital remittance (variable). The longest step is foreign document apostille which can take 7-21 days depending on the issuing country.
What is Form FC-GPR?
Foreign Currency - Gross Provisional Return. Mandatory FEMA filing to RBI within 30 days of receiving foreign share subscription money. Reports: shareholder details, remittance amount, equity issued, pricing method. Non-filing attracts FEMA compounding fee under Section 13. Core service we deliver as part of standard WOS setup engagement.
