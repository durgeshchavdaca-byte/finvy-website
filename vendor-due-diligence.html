Transaction Services

Vendor Due Diligence & Road to IPO

Whether you are selling a stake or heading for a listing, buyers and the market will test your numbers and your story. We build the vendor due-diligence pack and IPO-readiness foundation that withstands that scrutiny — and accelerates the deal.

Approach

How we work.

We diligence your own business as rigorously as a buyer or regulator will, so issues are fixed on your timeline, not theirs.

Step 01 / 05

Readiness review

Assess financial, commercial and governance readiness against buyer and listing standards.

Step 02 / 05

Diligence pack

Build the vendor due-diligence report — financials, market, operations and risks.

Step 03 / 05

Equity story

Shape the equity narrative and growth thesis the market will buy.

Step 04 / 05

Gap remediation

Close gaps in reporting, controls, governance and compliance.

Step 05 / 05

Process management

Manage the data room and advisor process through to transaction or listing.

Scope

What we cover.

What a VDD and IPO-readiness mandate covers.

  • Sell-side financial due diligence
  • Commercial and market due diligence
  • Quality-of-earnings analysis
  • Equity story and growth-thesis development
  • Governance, controls and compliance readiness
  • Financial reporting and restatement support
  • Data-room preparation and management
  • IPO-readiness roadmap and timeline
