US Incorporation for German Founders

For German founders building US-facing businesses, incorporating in Delaware unlocks US VC funding, US enterprise customers, payment rails (Stripe, etc.), and an eventual US exit. We are a CA-led advisory firm running US company formation end-to-end for German founders — from entity selection through ongoing US-Germany compliance.

Why incorporate in the US

For German founders, here's why.

Strategic reasons:

  • US VC funding access
  • US enterprise customer trust
  • Stripe/Mercury/Brex payment rails
  • Clean cap table for ESOPs and preferred stock
  • QSBS (Section 1202) — up to $10M tax-free gain
  • US IP protection (USPTO)
  • Easier global expansion from US parent
  • Tax-efficient exit

How we help

End-to-end for German founders.

What's included:

  • Entity selection — Delaware C-Corp vs LLC vs Wyoming
  • State of incorporation choice with reasoning
  • Certificate of Incorporation filing
  • EIN registration (no SSN needed)
  • Registered agent setup
  • Founders' stock + 83(b) elections
  • US bank account intro (Mercury/Brex/Wise)
  • Germany-side coordination: German GmbH, Finanzamt tax, Delaware C-Corp for US VC and SaaS sales
  • Year-1 + ongoing US filings (1120, 5472)
  • BOIR / CTA reporting
