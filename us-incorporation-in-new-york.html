US Incorporation in New York

New York incorporation — for businesses with actual New York operations. Expensive ($25 + publication requirement up to $2,000), state corporate tax 6.5-7.25%, but necessary for NYC-based businesses.

New York at a glance

Key facts.

All the numbers that matter when choosing a state.

CriterionNew York
State corporate tax6.5% (under $5M) to 7.25%
Annual franchise tax (min)$25 — minimum corporate franchise
Publication requirementYes — costly ($500-2,000 in NYC, lower upstate)
LLC fee$25 filing + $9-$4,500 annual based on revenue
Best forBusinesses with physical NYC presence, NY-based financial services

How to incorporate

The process.

What it takes to form an entity in New York.

  • Choose entity (C-Corp / LLC)
  • Reserve name with New York Secretary of State
  • Appoint registered agent in New York
  • File articles of incorporation/formation
  • Get EIN from IRS (Form SS-4)
  • Open US bank account
  • Comply with New York-specific requirements
  • Ongoing annual reports + franchise tax
