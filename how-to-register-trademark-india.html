How to Register a Trademark in India

Step-by-step guide to registering a trademark in India under the Trade Marks Act 1999. Online via IPIndia Public Search. Cost ₹4,500-9,000 per class.

Steps

The process.

How to register a trademark in india.

Step 01 / 08

Trademark search

Search IPIndia public search (https://ipindiaonline.gov.in) for similar/identical marks in your class. Avoid conflicts.

Step 02 / 08

Identify classes

India follows Nice Classification — 45 classes (34 goods, 11 services). Pick the right class(es) for your business.

Step 03 / 08

Decide priority claim

If filed in India within 6 months of foreign filing, claim priority (Article 4 Paris Convention). Brings foreign filing date.

Step 04 / 08

File TM-A (online)

File on ipindiaonline.gov.in. Pay: ₹4,500 for individual/startup, ₹9,000 for company. Per class. DSC required.

Step 05 / 08

Examination by IPIndia

2-4 months. Examiner issues report. Either accept or raise objections (Section 9 / 11).

Step 06 / 08

Respond to objections (if any)

Reply within 1 month. Common objections: descriptive, similar mark, lack of distinctiveness. We respond + appear in hearings.

Step 07 / 08

Publication in TM Journal

If accepted, mark published in weekly journal. 4-month opposition window.

Step 08 / 08

Registration

If no opposition (or opposition defeated), mark registered. Receive TM certificate. Valid 10 years, renewable.
