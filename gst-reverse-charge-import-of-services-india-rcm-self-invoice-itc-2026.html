GST Reverse Charge in India (2026): Import of Services, Legal, Security and Other Domestic RCM Services - When RCM Applies, Self-Invoice, Payment, GSTR-3B Reporting and ITC, the Indian buyer pays the GST, then claims it back.

When a business in India buys services from a supplier located outside India (software subscriptions, consulting, design, cloud, legal or marketing services, intra-group charges), the Indian recipient pays the GST under reverse charge, not the foreign supplier. The levy comes from section 5(3) of the IGST Act read with Notification 10/2017-Integrated Tax (Rate), which covers services supplied by a person in a non-taxable territory to a person in India other than a non-taxable online recipient. The recipient issues a self-invoice, pays IGST in cash, reports it in GSTR-3B Table 3.1(d), and can then claim the same amount as input tax credit if the service is used for business. Consumers buying online services are different: the foreign supplier must register and charge GST itself.

/ Does RCM apply to you?

Quick test.

SituationWho pays GST
Indian GST-registered business buys services from a foreign supplierThe Indian business, under reverse charge
Indian business receives services from its foreign parent or group company, with or without a chargeThe Indian business; services from a related person abroad can be a supply even without consideration
Indian consumer (not registered) buys online services (apps, streaming, SaaS) from abroadThe foreign supplier, through simplified OIDAR registration; see OIDAR GST
Service performed and consumed entirely outside India (place of supply outside India)Generally not an import of services; check the place-of-supply rules

/ What to do each time

Self-invoice, pay, report, claim.

  • Self-invoice: issue an invoice on yourself for the imported service, and a payment voucher when you pay.
  • Time of supply: generally the earlier of the payment date or 60 days from the foreign supplier's invoice; for associated enterprises, also watch the date of entry in your books.
  • Pay: IGST at the applicable rate (often 18%) in cash through the electronic cash ledger; reverse-charge tax cannot be paid using input credit.
  • Report: the liability in GSTR-3B Table 3.1(d) and the eligible credit in Table 4; keep the self-invoice and payment proof.
  • Also check withholding: income tax withholding on the payment to the foreign supplier, the treaty position and Forms 145/146 (formerly 15CA/15CB) are separate from GST.

Missed RCM on foreign software and services?

It is common, and fixable: pay the tax with interest, take the credit where eligible, and align it before the annual return or an audit picks it up. WhatsApp CA Durgesh Chavda.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is GST applicable on import of services?
Yes. Services imported by a business in India are taxed under reverse charge: the Indian recipient pays IGST, under section 5(3) of the IGST Act and Notification 10/2017-Integrated Tax (Rate).
Is RCM applicable on import of services?
Yes, when the supplier is located outside India and the recipient is in India (other than a non-taxable online recipient). The recipient pays the tax and can claim credit if eligible.
Is GST charged on Google Ads, and is it reverse charge?
For advertisers in India, Google Ads is billed by Google's Indian entity, which charges GST at 18% on the invoice under forward charge, so it is not an import of services and no reverse charge applies. Add your GSTIN and registered address in the Google Ads billing settings so the invoice carries it, and you can claim the GST as input tax credit if the ads are for your business. Ads bought from a foreign platform that bills you from abroad without Indian GST are different: a registered business pays that GST itself under reverse charge as an import of services. Book a call to check your ad and software invoices for missed credit or reverse charge.
Which notification covers reverse charge on import of services?
Notification No. 10/2017-Integrated Tax (Rate) dated 28 June 2017, entry 1.
Can reverse-charge GST be paid from input tax credit?
No. It must be paid in cash through the electronic cash ledger; the amount paid can then be claimed as input tax credit if the service is used for business.
Which domestic services attract GST under reverse charge - legal, security, directors, rent?
Notification 13/2017-Central Tax (Rate) lists the domestic services on which the recipient business, not the supplier, pays GST. The common ones: legal services by an advocate or a firm of advocates to a business entity (businesses below the registration threshold are exempt); security services (supply of security personnel) by a supplier other than a company to a registered person; services of a director who is not an employee to the company; goods transport agency services where the GTA has not opted to charge GST itself; sponsorship services to a company or firm; insurance agent and recovery agent services to insurers and banks; and renting of commercial property by an unregistered landlord to a registered tenant other than a composition dealer. The recipient pays the tax in cash, reports it in GSTR-3B Table 3.1(d), issues a self-invoice where the supplier is unregistered, and claims the input tax credit if eligible. Is GST payable on salary costs recharged by a foreign parent for seconded employees?
It depends on who is the employer in substance, and the law here is not settled. In 2022 the Supreme Court held, under service tax, that a foreign group company seconding staff to its Indian affiliate was supplying manpower, taxable in India under reverse charge, even though the Indian company paid the salaries locally - and departments have applied the same reasoning under GST, treating the recharge as an import of services on which the Indian entity pays IGST under reverse charge. CBIC Circular 210/4/2024 then clarified that where the Indian entity is eligible for full input tax credit, the value declared in the invoice - or nil, where no invoice is issued - can be taken as the value of services from a related foreign entity, and later High Court rulings have gone different ways on the facts. Because the reverse-charge tax is usually creditable, the main risks are cash flow, interest and penalties on past periods. Review the secondment agreement, who controls and can dismiss the employees, how salary, social security and costs flow, and the TDS and transfer pricing position together. The tax liability in Table 3.1(d) (inward supplies liable to reverse charge) and the eligible credit in Table 4.
