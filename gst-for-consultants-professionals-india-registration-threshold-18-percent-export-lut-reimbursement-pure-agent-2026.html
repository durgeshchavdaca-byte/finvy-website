/ Consultants, freelancers, advisers, designers and other professionals · GST on professional services · Updated 2026-11-28

GST for Consultants and Professionals in India (2026): When to Register, the 18% Rate, Foreign Clients and LUT, Composition at 6%, Reverse Charge and GST on Reimbursements, register at the right time, bill it the right way.

A consultant or professional needs GST registration once turnover crosses INR 20 lakh (INR 10 lakh in some special category states), or earlier by choice. Most professional services are taxed at 18%; services to foreign clients that meet the export conditions are zero-rated under an LUT; small service providers can opt for composition at 6%. Expenses you recharge to clients usually attract GST too - unless you incur them strictly as a pure agent.

The basics for professionals.

  • Voluntary registration: worth considering if business clients want to claim input tax credit or you export services and want refunds.
  • Aggregate turnover: counts all your supplies across India under the same PAN, including exempt and export supplies.

/ GST on reimbursement of expenses

Pure agent or part of your fee?

  • General rule: travel, hotel, courier and other expenses you recharge to a client are part of the value of your service and attract GST at the same rate.
  • Pure agent exception (rule 33): expenses are excluded only if you act as the client’s pure agent under a written agreement, pay on the client’s behalf, show them separately on the invoice, recover them at actual, and the client receives the supply you procured (for example government fees paid in the client’s name).
  • Invoices: show fees, taxable reimbursements and pure agent recoveries as separate lines.
  • Input tax credit: a registered consultant can claim credit on business purchases, except blocked items.
  • Returns: monthly or quarterly returns (QRMP), plus annual return where applicable.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Do consultants need GST registration in India?
Yes, once aggregate turnover crosses INR 20 lakh for services (INR 10 lakh in some special category states). Below that, registration is optional - many register voluntarily for business clients or to export services under an LUT.
What is the GST rate for consultancy and professional services?
Most professional and consulting services are taxed at 18%. Some services have special treatment - for example legal services by advocates to businesses are under reverse charge, and health care by doctors is exempt.
Is GST charged on consulting services to foreign clients?
Services that meet the export of services conditions are zero-rated. You can supply under a Letter of Undertaking without paying IGST, or pay IGST and claim a refund, with payment received in convertible foreign exchange or as RBI permits.
Is GST applicable on reimbursement of expenses?
Generally yes - expenses recharged to a client are part of the value of the service. They are excluded only when you act as a pure agent under rule 33: a written agreement, payment on the client's behalf, separate disclosure on the invoice, recovery at actual, and the client receiving the procured supply.
Can a consultant use the GST composition scheme?
Yes, eligible service providers with turnover up to INR 50 lakh in the previous year can pay 6% under the services composition scheme, without input tax credit and without charging GST to clients.
