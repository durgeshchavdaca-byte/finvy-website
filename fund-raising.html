SEED TO SERIES D

Fund Raising

From pre-seed to Series D, we structure and execute capital raises that match your stage, sector, and ambition - connecting you with the right investors across India, US, UAE, and UK.

Process

How we work.

A structured engagement — from first conversation to delivered outcome. Each mandate follows a transparent process so founders always know what's next.

Step 01 / 05

Discovery

We assess your business model, unit economics, runway, and capital needs.

Step 02 / 05

Structuring

Design the deal architecture: instrument terms, cap table impact, valuation framework.

Step 03 / 05

Materials

Build investment-grade financial models, pitch decks, data rooms, and collateral packages.

Step 04 / 05

Matchmaking

Activate our curated network of domestic and international investors.

Step 05 / 05

Negotiation & Close

Lead term sheet negotiations, optimize SHA/SPA terms, and manage closing end-to-end.

Scope

What we cover.

The full range of expertise we bring to every engagement in this practice.

  • Equity fundraising - Pre-Seed, Seed, Series A through Series D
  • Venture debt advisory - term loans, revenue-based financing, working capital lines
  • SAFE, convertible notes, and hybrid instrument structuring
  • Angel investor and HNI syndicate coordination
  • VC, PE, and family office investor matchmaking across geographies
  • Pitch deck design, financial model, and data room preparation
  • Term sheet negotiation, SHA/SSA drafting, and investor alignment
  • Cap table restructuring, dilution modeling, and waterfall analysis
  • Valuation services - DCF, comparable, precedent, and 409A
  • Cross-border capital structuring under FEMA, ODI, and ECB frameworks
  • Bridge round and mezzanine financing advisory
  • Post-close investor relations and board advisory setup
