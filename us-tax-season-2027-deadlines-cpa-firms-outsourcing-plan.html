US Tax Season 2027 Deadlines for CPA Firms, and when to line up help.

For tax year 2026, W-2s and 1099-NECs are due on 1 February 2027, partnership and S corporation returns on 15 March, and individual and C corporation returns and FBARs on 15 April, with extensions to 15 September and 15 October. Firms that want outsourced capacity should set it up in November and December, before the season starts.

/ The 2027 filing-season dates

Calendar-year returns for tax year 2026.

Return (calendar-year filers)DueExtended due date
Forms W-2 and 1099-NEC1 February 2027 (31 January falls on a Sunday)—
Form 1065 (partnerships) and Form 1120-S (S corporations)15 March 202715 September 2027 (Form 7004)
Form 1040 (individuals)15 April 202715 October 2027 (Form 4868)
Form 1120 (C corporations), including pro forma 1120 with Form 5472 for foreign-owned LLCs15 April 202715 October 2027 (Form 7004)
FBAR (FinCEN Form 114)15 April 202715 October 2027 (automatic)

An extension extends the time to file, not to pay. Fiscal-year entities follow their own dates.

/ When to line up outsourced help

Work back from the deadlines.

  1. November to December 2026: choose the provider, sign the confidentiality agreement, add the third-party provider clause to engagement letters, add the Section 7216 offshore consent to organizers, update the written information security plan, set up remote access and test with sample returns.
  2. January 2027: W-2 and 1099 preparation, year-end bookkeeping clean-up and reconciliations.
  3. February to mid-March 2027: Forms 1065 and 1120-S, K-1s.
  4. Mid-March to mid-April 2027: Forms 1040 and 1120, Form 5472, FBARs; extensions for the rest.
  5. May to October 2027: extended returns, with peaks before 15 September and 15 October.

Firms that start onboarding in January usually lose the first weeks of the season to access and consent paperwork.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

When are 2026 partnership and S corporation returns due?
Calendar-year Forms 1065 and 1120-S are due on 15 March 2027, or 15 September 2027 with a Form 7004 extension.
When are 2026 individual returns due?
Form 1040 is due on 15 April 2027, or 15 October 2027 with Form 4868. Tax is still due by 15 April.
When are 1099-NEC forms due for 2026?
1 February 2027, because 31 January 2027 falls on a Sunday.
When should a CPA firm set up outsourcing for busy season?
In November or December, so that consents, engagement letters, the security plan and system access are ready before January.
