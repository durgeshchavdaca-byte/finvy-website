Stripe Atlas vs doola: which is better for Indian founders

Comparing Stripe Atlas and doola on price, scope, EIN timelines and post-incorporation compliance bundling for non-resident founders.

Overview

Stripe Atlas vs doola: which is better for Indian founders

Both Stripe Atlas and doola pitch hassle-free US company formation for international founders, but they target different segments. Atlas is built around the Stripe payments ecosystem and prioritises Delaware C-Corp formation for venture-backed startups. doola serves a broader audience including LLC formation, sole proprietors, e-commerce sellers and freelancers, and bundles ongoing tax and bookkeeping plans. Indian founders need to match the platform to the legal structure they actually want.

Entity types and structure

C-Corp focus vs LLC flexibility

Stripe Atlas defaults to Delaware C-Corp because that is the structure VCs require for SAFE and priced rounds. It also offers Delaware LLC formation. doola supports Delaware, Wyoming and Florida LLCs, Delaware C-Corp, and DBA (sole proprietorship) registrations across multiple states. For an Indian founder who wants a Wyoming LLC for an e-commerce or content business (no franchise tax, lower fees, simpler reporting), doola is the natural fit. For a venture-backed startup, Atlas is purpose-built.

Pricing and bundling

One-time vs recurring

Stripe Atlas charges around USD 500 one-time for C-Corp formation with EIN, post-formation documents and registered agent for year one. doola's Starter plan covers LLC formation at around USD 297 plus state fee; the Total Compliance plan at around USD 1,999 per year bundles formation, EIN, registered agent, annual report, BOI filing, ITIN application support, US business bank account and bookkeeping software. Atlas treats compliance as referrals; doola sells compliance as a subscription. Pick based on whether you want to assemble the stack yourself or buy a bundle.

Post-incorporation services

ITIN, bookkeeping, tax filing

doola heavily markets ITIN application as part of higher tiers - useful for Indian founders who need US tax filing capability, want to claim treaty benefits or want personal US banking access. doola also bundles QuickBooks-equivalent bookkeeping and tax filing (Forms 1120, 5472, 1065 as applicable). Stripe Atlas does not bundle these; it refers to partner CAs and tools. For a founder who wants one vendor for formation through year-end tax filing, doola's subscription is the cleaner option, even if the all-in price is higher.

Pick X if, pick Y if

When each is the right call

Pick Stripe Atlas if: you are building a C-Corp for venture investment, you intend to use Stripe payments, you have or can hire a CA for ongoing tax work, and you want the lowest one-time formation cost. Pick doola if: you want an LLC (Wyoming, Delaware or Florida), you need ITIN help, you want bookkeeping and tax filing bundled into one subscription, or you run an e-commerce or services business that does not require a venture-grade C-Corp. Both can incorporate you correctly; the difference is what comes after Day 1.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can doola incorporate a Delaware C-Corp like Stripe Atlas can?
Yes. doola supports Delaware C-Corp formation in addition to LLCs. The C-Corp formation flow is comparable, but Atlas remains more focused on the venture-backed C-Corp use case with template SAFE documents.

Does doola handle ITIN applications?
Yes, in higher tiers. ITIN application via Form W-7 is bundled into doola's Total Compliance plan. Atlas does not provide ITIN service. Independent CAA-certified agents can also file W-7s.

Which platform is better for an LLC?
doola has deeper LLC support including Wyoming, Delaware and Florida, with bundled compliance for ongoing annual reports and BOI filings. Atlas can form a Delaware LLC but is optimised for C-Corp.

Do either handle Form 5472?
doola's compliance tier includes Form 5472 and Form 1120 preparation for foreign-owned single-member LLCs. Atlas does not file these; you need a CA. Form 5472 is mandatory for foreign-owned US entities even with zero income.

How fast can I be operational?
Both can form the entity in days. Bank account and EIN are the gates. For Indian founders without ITIN, EIN takes four to eight weeks via fax. Plan launch dates accordingly regardless of platform.

Is doola actually cheaper over three years?
Atlas is cheaper if you DIY ongoing compliance. doola's bundled plan is more expensive headline but can be cheaper than buying registered agent, bookkeeping, tax filing and ITIN service separately. Run the actual numbers for your year-three cost.
