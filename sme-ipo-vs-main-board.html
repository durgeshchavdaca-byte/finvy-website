SME IPO vs Main Board IPO

Should your company list on the SME platform (BSE SME / NSE Emerge) or go directly for a Main Board IPO? Here is the CA-led comparison for Indian founders weighing the choice.

Side-by-side

Quick comparison.

SME IPO vs Main Board IPO for Indian companies.

CriterionSME IPOMain Board IPO
PlatformBSE SME, NSE EmergeBSE Main, NSE Main
Min post-issue paid-up capital≥ ₹1 crore≥ ₹10 crore
Max post-issue paid-up capitalUp to ₹25 croreNo upper limit
Profitability requirementOperating profits in 2 of last 3 FYs (simpler)Net tangible assets ≥ ₹3cr in 3 FYs, ₹15cr PAT avg in 3 of 5 FYs
Issue routeFixed price or Book BuildingBook Building (primarily)
DRHP filing withStock exchange (BSE / NSE)SEBI
Approval timeline8–10 weeks (exchange)20–24 weeks (SEBI)
Minimum subscribers50 allottees1,000 allottees
Underwriting100% mandatory100% mandatory
Market makingMandatory for 3 yearsNot mandatory
Total cost (typical)₹40 lakh – ₹1.5 crore₹2 crore – ₹15 crore+
Best forProfitable SMEs raising ₹10–50 crore for growth, expansion, working capitalMature companies raising ₹100 crore+ with broad investor base
MigrationCan migrate to Main Board after meeting criteriaN/A
