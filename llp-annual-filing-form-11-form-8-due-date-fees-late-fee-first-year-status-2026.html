/ LLP partners and founders · MCA compliance · Updated 2026-11-25

LLP Legal Requirements and Annual Filing (2026): Form 11 by 30 May, Form 8 by 30 October, Late Fees and Penalty, First-Year Filing, Status Check and Strike-Off (Form 24), two MCA forms and one tax return, every year, even with no business.

Every LLP in India files two annual forms with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs and an income tax return, whether or not it did any business. Form 11 (annual return of partners and contributions) is due by 30 May, within 60 days of the year-end. Form 8 (statement of account and solvency) is due by 30 October. The income tax return is ITR-5. The MCA’s 2026 extension (General Circular 05/2026) covered only company forms AOC-4 and MGT-7, not LLP forms, so a Form 8 for FY 2025-26 filed after 30 October 2026 already carries additional fees that grow with the delay.

/ Quick answers

What LLP partners search for.

  • What are the LLP annual filing forms? Form 11 (annual return) and Form 8 (statement of account and solvency) with the MCA, plus ITR-5 with the income tax department.
  • What is the last date? Form 11 by 30 May and Form 8 by 30 October, each year, for the financial year ended 31 March.
  • Is filing needed if the LLP had no business? Yes. Nil-activity LLPs still file Form 11, Form 8 and ITR-5. An LLP that wants to stop must apply to be struck off, not just stop filing.
  • What is the purpose of Form 11? It reports the designated partners and partners, their contributions and changes during the year. Form 8 reports the financial position and the partners’ statement that the LLP is solvent.
  • Is an audit compulsory? Accounts must be audited if turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh or contribution exceeds ₹25 lakh in the year. A separate income tax audit applies above the income tax turnover limits.
  • How do I check filing status? On the MCA portal, use View Company / LLP Master Data with the LLPIN to see the latest filing dates, or check the LLP’s filings in the public documents service.

/ First year and special cases

Where people get stuck.

  • First year: an LLP incorporated after 30 September can close its first financial year on 31 March of the following year, so its first Form 11 and Form 8 fall in the year after that. An LLP incorporated earlier in the year files for the year ending the next 31 March.
  • Overdue years: file the oldest years first; each form carries its own additional fee. Long default can lead the Registrar to treat the LLP as inactive and start strike-off.
  • Foreign or NRI partners: the same forms apply; foreign contribution also needs FEMA reporting (Form LLP-I / LLP-II through the RBI’s FIRMS portal). See LLP registration with NRI or foreign partners.
  • Moving to a company: see converting an LLP to a private limited company and LLP vs private limited.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is the due date for LLP annual filing?
Form 11 (annual return) is due by 30 May and Form 8 (statement of account and solvency) by 30 October each year. The income tax return, ITR-5, follows the income tax due dates.
What are the legal requirements for an LLP in India?
From the start: at least two designated partners with DINs, at least one of them resident in India (stayed in India for at least 120 days in the financial year, after the 2021 amendment), and the LLP agreement filed in Form 3 within 30 days of incorporation. Every year: Form 11 (annual return) by 30 May, Form 8 (statement of account and solvency) by 30 October, ITR-5 by the income tax due date, books of account kept on a cash or accrual basis, and an audit of the accounts once turnover exceeds Rs 40 lakh or contribution exceeds Rs 25 lakh. Designated partners file DIR-3 KYC to keep their DINs active. On changes: a new or exiting partner or designated partner is reported in Form 4, and any change to the LLP agreement in Form 3, each within 30 days. GST registration, TDS and any FEMA reporting for foreign partners apply on top where relevant. Is the LLP Form 8 due date for FY 2025-26 extended?
No. MCA General Circular 05/2026 extended only the company forms AOC-4 and MGT-7. LLP Form 8 for FY 2025-26 was due on 30 October 2026 and additional fees apply after that.
What is the late fee for LLP annual filing?
An additional fee that is a multiple of the normal fee for each late form: from 1x for up to 15 days to 15x for small LLPs or 30x for others after 180 days, plus a per-day amount beyond 360 days.
What is the penalty for not filing LLP annual returns?
Two things apply. First, the additional fee on each late form: a multiple of the normal filing fee that rises with the delay, up to 15 times for a small LLP or 30 times for other LLPs, plus Rs 10 or Rs 20 a day beyond 360 days. Second, the LLP Act penalty: for a late annual return (Form 11) under section 35, Rs 100 a day, up to Rs 1 lakh for the LLP and Rs 50,000 for each designated partner. MCA General Circular 05/2026 does not cover LLP forms, so filing the pending forms now stops both from growing.

Does an LLP with no business need to file returns?
Yes. An LLP with no activity still files Form 11, Form 8 and ITR-5 every year until it is struck off.
How do I strike off or close an LLP?
An LLP that has not carried on business for a year or more can apply to the Registrar in Form 24 under rule 37 of the LLP Rules, with the consent of all partners. Before filing, the overdue Form 8 and Form 11 up to the end of the financial year in which it stopped business must be filed. Form 24 is usually supported by a statement of accounts showing no assets or liabilities, affidavits and an indemnity by the designated partners, and the partners' authority - check the current MCA instruction kit for the attachment list. Close the bank account, surrender GST registration and settle tax filings first. Separately, the Registrar can strike off an LLP on its own if it has not carried on business for two years or more.

When is an LLP audit compulsory?
When turnover exceeds Rs 40 lakh or partners' contribution exceeds Rs 25 lakh in the financial year. Income tax audit applies separately above the income tax turnover limits.
Can an LLP partner take a salary, and what is the maximum remuneration?
Yes, if the LLP agreement authorises it and it is paid to a working partner who is an individual. For tax, the LLP can deduct remuneration up to the section 40(b) limit: on the first INR 6 lakh of book profit (or a loss), the higher of INR 3 lakh or 90% of book profit, and 60% of the balance, from AY 2025-26. Interest on partners' capital is deductible up to 12% simple a year. Remuneration and interest are taxed in the partner's hands as business income, and the LLP deducts TDS at 10% under section 194T once payments to a partner exceed INR 20,000 in the year.
