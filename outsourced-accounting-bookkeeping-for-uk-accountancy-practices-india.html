/ Making Tax Digital for Income Tax

The workload shift.

  • From 6 April 2026: sole traders and landlords with qualifying income above GBP 50,000 (based on 2024-25) keep digital records and send quarterly updates.
  • From April 2027 the threshold falls to GBP 30,000, and from April 2028 to GBP 20,000.
  • Partnerships are not yet in scope.
  • Quarterly updates multiply bookkeeping touchpoints; an offshore team can take the routine processing while your staff advise.

/ Data protection

UK GDPR when work goes to India.

  • A written processor contract is required under Article 28 of the UK GDPR.
  • India does not have a UK adequacy regulation, so a restricted transfer needs the International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) or the UK Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses, plus a transfer risk assessment.
  • Working only inside your systems through remote access, with multi-factor authentication and no local downloads, reduces risk.
  • Tell clients in your engagement terms and privacy notice that you use an outsourced provider.

Sources: ICO: international data transfer agreement; ACCA: data protection when extending your team; GOV.UK: Making Tax Digital for Income Tax.

Can UK accountants outsource bookkeeping to India?
Yes, with an Article 28 processor contract, the IDTA or UK Addendum for the transfer, a transfer risk assessment, and clear client disclosure.
What is the MTD for Income Tax threshold?
Qualifying income above GBP 50,000 from 6 April 2026, GBP 30,000 from April 2027 and GBP 20,000 from April 2028.

Xero, QuickBooks, Sage and your practice's tax software, accessed through your environment.
