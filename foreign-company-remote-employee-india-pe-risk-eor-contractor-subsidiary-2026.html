Foreign Company With Remote Employees in India: PE Risk, Payroll Tax and EOR vs Contractor vs Subsidiary (2026), one hire can create an Indian tax presence.

A US, UK, Singapore or UAE company can hire people in India without an Indian entity, but it should choose the route deliberately. Salary for work done in India is taxable in India wherever it is paid, and the Supreme Court has held that the payer's duty to withhold tax extends to salary paid abroad for Indian services. The bigger risk is a permanent establishment (PE): if someone in India habitually concludes contracts for you, or works from premises at your disposal, India can tax part of your profits. The three common routes are an employer of record (EOR), independent contractors, and an Indian subsidiary.

/ Where the PE risk comes from

Treaty and facts decide it.

  • Dependent agent: a person in India who habitually concludes contracts, or plays the principal role in concluding them, for the foreign company. Keep contract authority outside India.
  • Fixed place: an office or premises at the company's disposal. An employee's home office is usually not at the company's disposal, but India has reservations on the OECD's 2025 home-office guidance, so document the arrangement.
  • Service PE: under some treaties, services furnished through personnel in India beyond a period; Indian tribunals have required physical presence of personnel (Delhi ITAT, 2024).
  • Senior management in India: if key decisions are taken from India, ask whether the company's place of effective management could shift.

/ Moving to a subsidiary

When and how.

  • Triggers: a growing team, Indian customers, people in sales or management roles, or a fundraising or acquisition due diligence that will ask about India.
  • Set-up: a private limited company with foreign shareholding under the automatic route for most sectors, share capital received through banking channels and Form FC-GPR reported after allotment.
  • Ongoing: transfer pricing on the service fee charged to the parent, annual Form 3CEB, the annual return on foreign liabilities and assets (FLA), and payroll compliance.
  • Moving staff over: transfer employees from the EOR or contractor arrangement with continuity of service terms agreed in writing.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Can a US company hire an employee in India without an Indian entity?
Yes, through an employer of record or as an independent contractor, or directly with care. Salary for work done in India is taxable in India, and withholding obligations can extend to salary paid abroad, so the route should be chosen deliberately.
Does one remote employee in India create a permanent establishment?
Not automatically. The risk depends on the treaty and what the person does: habitually concluding contracts for the company, or working from premises at the company's disposal, are the main triggers. Engineering roles carry less risk than sales roles.
Is an employer of record enough to avoid PE?
An EOR handles employment and payroll, but PE risk still depends on the activities of the people in India. Keep contract authority outside India and document roles.
When should we set up an Indian subsidiary?
Usually when the team grows past a handful, when you sell to Indian customers, when India staff hold sales or management roles, or before a fundraising or acquisition due diligence.
What compliance does an Indian subsidiary of a foreign company need?
FDI reporting on share allotment (Form FC-GPR), transfer pricing on charges to the parent with an annual Form 3CEB, the FLA return, corporate tax and payroll compliance.
