AIF & VCF SETUP

Fund Structuring & Alternatives

We help set up and structure private equity, venture capital, and alternative investment funds across key jurisdictions. From fund domiciliation strategy and GP/LP alignment through regulatory registration, investor onboarding, and exit - we cover the full fund lifecycle with precision.

Process

How we work.

A structured engagement — from first conversation to delivered outcome. Each mandate follows a transparent process so founders always know what's next.

Step 01 / 04

Assess

Evaluate fund strategy, target AUM, and investor base.

Step 02 / 04

Structure

Design GP/LP framework, waterfall, and governance.

Step 03 / 04

Register

File with relevant regulators (SEBI, MAS, FSC, IFSCA).

Step 04 / 04

Launch

Prepare PPM, onboard investors, and begin operations.

Scope

What we cover.

The full range of expertise we bring to every engagement in this practice.

  • Private equity and venture capital fund setup - India, GIFT City, Singapore, Mauritius
  • GP/LP structuring and investor onboarding documentation (PPM, side letters)
  • Family office structuring and cross-border asset vehicles
  • Fund domiciliation strategy and global jurisdiction comparison
  • Fund taxation strategy, carried interest design, and distribution waterfall modeling
  • Regulatory registration - SEBI, MAS, FSC, IFSCA frameworks
  • Exit strategy, fund unwinding, and regulatory closure support
