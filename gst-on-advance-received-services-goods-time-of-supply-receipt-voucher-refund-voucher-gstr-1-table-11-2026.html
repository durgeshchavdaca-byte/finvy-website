/ Service providers, contractors and suppliers taking customer advances · GST time of supply · Updated 2026-11-29

GST on Advance Received (2026): Services vs Goods, Time of Supply, Receipt Voucher, Refund Voucher and Reporting Advances in GSTR-1, services pay on the advance, goods usually do not.

GST is payable on an advance received for services, because the time of supply is the earlier of the invoice or the payment. For goods, registered suppliers (other than composition taxpayers) do not pay GST on advances - the tax is paid when the invoice is issued. Either way, issue a receipt voucher for the advance, report it in GSTR-1, and adjust it when you invoice.

/ When GST applies to advances

Sections 12 and 13 and Notification 66/2017-Central Tax.

SituationGST on the advance
Advance for services (including works contracts)Payable - time of supply is the earlier of invoice and payment
Advance for goods (regular registered supplier)Not payable - tax is paid when the invoice is issued
Advance received by a composition taxpayer for goodsThe exemption for advances does not cover composition taxpayers
Rate or nature of supply not known at the timePay at 18%; treat as inter-state if the nature is not known
Excess of up to INR 1,000 over the invoiceSupplier may treat the invoice date as the time of supply for the excess
  • Receipt voucher: issue one for every advance, showing the amount, rate and tax.
  • Reporting: show advances in GSTR-1 (Table 11) and adjust them in the month the invoice is raised, with GSTR-3B matching.

/ Cancellations, refunds and common errors

Get the trail right.

  • Supply cancelled: if no invoice is issued and the advance is returned, issue a refund voucher and adjust the tax paid on the advance in the return.
  • Export advances: advances for export of services under an LUT carry no tax, but the export conditions must still be met when the service is supplied.
  • Long-term contracts: milestone advances on service contracts trigger tax as each advance is received.
  • Notices: departments compare advances in bank statements and books with tax paid - unpaid tax on service advances attracts interest.
  • Input tax credit: the recipient can claim credit only after the supply is received and the invoice is issued, not on the receipt voucher.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is GST payable on advance received for services?
Yes. For services, the time of supply is the earliest of the invoice date, the date payment is received or the date of provision, so GST is due on the advance in the month it is received. Issue a receipt voucher and report it in GSTR-1.
Is GST payable on advance received for goods?
No, for regular registered suppliers. Notification 66/2017-Central Tax removed the liability on advances for goods, so tax is paid when the invoice is issued. The exemption does not cover composition taxpayers.
What rate applies if the rate is not known when the advance is received?
Tax is paid at 18%, and if the nature of supply is not known it is treated as an inter-state supply.
What happens if the advance is refunded and the supply never happens?
Issue a refund voucher and adjust the tax paid on the advance in the return for the period.
Is GST payable on advance received for a works contract?
Yes. A works contract is a supply of services, so GST is payable on each advance when it is received.
