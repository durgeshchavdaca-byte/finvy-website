IFSC & AIF LICENSING

GIFT City & IFSC Advisory

GIFT City's International Financial Services Centre (IFSC) offers a compelling regulatory and tax environment for funds, fintechs, and global holding structures. With a unified regulator (IFSCA), 10-year tax holidays, zero GST, and access to global capital markets, it's India's gateway to international finance.

Process

How we work.

A structured engagement — from first conversation to delivered outcome. Each mandate follows a transparent process so founders always know what's next.

Step 01 / 04

Evaluate

Assess if GIFT City is right for your business model.

Step 02 / 04

Structure

Design the optimal entity type and licensing path.

Step 03 / 04

Register

File with IFSCA and complete all regulatory formalities.

Step 04 / 04

Operate

Set up banking, office, and ongoing compliance systems.

Scope

What we cover.

The full range of expertise we bring to every engagement in this practice.

  • AIF setup and structuring - Category I, II, III under IFSCA regulations
  • IFSC banking, FinTech, and insurance entity licensing
  • SPV structuring for global investments and international holding entities
  • Qualified Jewellers Registration under IIBX framework
  • End-to-end advisory for GIFT City entity setup, banking, and ongoing compliance
  • ECB and ODI structuring via IFSC framework for cross-border capital flows
