VRS Compensation Tax in India (2026): The INR 5 Lakh Exemption, Commuted Pension, Gratuity, Leave Encashment and the Deductions That Matter in Your Exit Year, plan the exit year before the money lands.

Compensation under a voluntary retirement scheme (VRS) is tax-free up to INR 5 lakh, once in a lifetime, if the scheme meets the prescribed conditions; the rest is taxed as salary. Gratuity, leave encashment and commuted pension have their own exemptions, which are also available under the new regime. Because the whole settlement lands in one year, the choice between the VRS exemption and section 89 relief, and between regimes, can change the tax noticeably.

/ How each exit payment is taxed

Retirement and VRS exemptions.

PaymentTax treatment
VRS compensation (scheme meeting rule 2BA)Exempt up to INR 5 lakh, once in a lifetime; balance taxed as salary
GratuityGovernment employees fully exempt; others exempt up to INR 20 lakh under the prescribed formula
Leave encashment on retirement or resignationGovernment employees fully exempt; others exempt up to INR 25 lakh
Commuted pensionGovernment employees fully exempt; others one-third exempt if gratuity is also received, one-half if not
Monthly (uncommuted) pensionTaxable as salary
Arrears or advance salaryRelief under section 89 via Form 10E - but not on VRS compensation if the 5 lakh exemption is claimed
  • New regime: the gratuity, leave encashment, commuted pension and VRS exemptions remain available.
  • New Act: the same exemptions continue in the Income-tax Act, 2025 from tax year 2026-27, under new section numbers.

/ Deductions and planning in the exit year

Get the year right.

  • Standard deduction: INR 75,000 under the new regime from FY 2024-25; INR 50,000 under the old regime.
  • Employer NPS (80CCD(2)): employer contribution deductible up to 14% of salary under the new regime from FY 2024-25; 10% for non-government employees under the old regime.
  • VRS exemption or section 89: compare both - spreading the compensation back over earlier years can sometimes save more than the 5 lakh exemption.
  • Regime choice: recheck the old vs new regime for the exit year; a large settlement can change the answer.
  • Check Form 16: employers sometimes miss exemptions on the final settlement - you can claim them in your return.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is VRS compensation taxable in India?
Up to INR 5 lakh is exempt, once in a lifetime, if the scheme meets the prescribed conditions (rule 2BA). Any amount above INR 5 lakh is taxed as salary in the year received.
Can I claim section 89 relief on VRS compensation?
Only if you do not claim the INR 5 lakh VRS exemption for the same compensation. Compare both: spreading the compensation back over earlier years under section 89 can sometimes give more relief.
How much gratuity is tax-free for private sector employees?
Up to INR 20 lakh, worked out under the prescribed formula. Gratuity received by government employees is fully exempt.
Is commuted pension taxable?
For government employees it is fully exempt. For others, one-third of the full commuted value is exempt if gratuity is also received, and one-half if it is not. Monthly pension is taxable as salary.
From which year does the INR 75,000 standard deduction apply?
From FY 2024-25 (AY 2025-26) under the new tax regime. The old regime continues with INR 50,000.
