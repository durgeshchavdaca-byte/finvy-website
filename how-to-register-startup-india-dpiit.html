How to Register for Startup India DPIIT

Step-by-step guide to register your startup with DPIIT under Startup India scheme. Free. Unlocks Section 80-IAC tax exemption, angel tax relief, IPR rebates.

Steps

The process.

How to register for startup india dpiit.

Step 01 / 08

Verify eligibility

Indian entity (Pvt Ltd / LLP / Partnership); incorporated < 10 years; turnover < ₹100cr in any FY; working on innovation/scalability.

Step 02 / 08

Create Startup India account

Sign up at startupindia.gov.in with founder email.

Step 03 / 08

Complete startup profile

Add company details, industry, services, founders, funding history (if any), team size.

Step 04 / 08

Upload incorporation docs

Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, brief about business (max 250 words), intellectual property (if any).

Step 05 / 08

Submit for DPIIT recognition

Click 'Get Recognised'. Application goes to DPIIT review queue.

Step 06 / 08

Wait for approval

Typical 2-7 days. DPIIT reviews and grants recognition certificate via email.

Step 07 / 08

Download DPIIT certificate

Save certificate PDF. Bears DPIIT recognition number — quote it in all future applications.

Step 08 / 08

Apply for Section 80-IAC (optional)

Separate application to Inter-Ministerial Board (IMB) for income tax exemption (3 of 7 years).
