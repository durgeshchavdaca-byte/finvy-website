Definition

What is Stripe Atlas?

Stripe Atlas is a US incorporation service from Stripe (the payment processor). It packages Delaware C-Corp formation + EIN + Mercury bank intro + Stripe payments + legal templates into a single $500 service. Popular with international founders, but limited on ongoing compliance.

What's included

What's included.

  • Delaware C-Corp incorporation
  • EIN registration
  • Mercury bank account intro
  • Stripe payments account
  • Standard founder stock issuance
  • Stock plan template (ESOP)
  • First-year tax filing reminder
  • Discounts on partner services

What's NOT included

What's missing.

  • Ongoing tax filings (Form 1120, 5472) — pay extra to partners
  • India-side compliance (FEMA, ODI, RBI reporting) — none
  • Custom legal advice — generic templates only
  • Transfer pricing for India-US structure — none
  • ESOP plan customisation — basic template
  • Advice on entity choice for your specific situation

Cost

Stripe Atlas cost.

  • $500 one-time fee
  • $100/year recurring (registered agent renewal)
  • Add-on tax filings: $1,000-3,000/year
  • Total Year 1 (with tax filings): $1,500-3,500
