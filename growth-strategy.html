Strategy

Growth Strategy & Market Landscaping

Where to play and how to win — answered with numbers, not opinions. We map the size and shape of your market, locate the white space competitors have missed, and translate it into a sequenced, costed plan your team can actually execute.

Approach

How we work.

Every growth mandate starts with the market and ends with a plan management can run on Monday morning. We move from evidence to options to a single recommended path.

Step 01 / 05

Market sizing

We size the addressable opportunity bottom-up — demand drivers, segments, and realistic capture rates — so targets are grounded, not aspirational.

Step 02 / 05

Competitive mapping

Map the players, their economics, and where each is strong or exposed, to find the lanes where you can win profitably.

Step 03 / 05

White-space scan

Identify under-served segments, geographies, and use-cases that fit your right-to-win.

Step 04 / 05

Option design

Build and pressure-test three to four growth options against capital, capability, and timing.

Step 05 / 05

Roadmap

Convert the chosen path into a phased, costed roadmap with owners, milestones, and the metrics that matter.

Scope

What we cover.

The analysis and decision support we bring to a growth mandate.

  • Total and serviceable market sizing (TAM / SAM / SOM)
  • Demand-driver and segment-level growth modelling
  • Competitive landscape and economics benchmarking
  • White-space and adjacency identification
  • Geographic and category expansion assessment
  • Right-to-win and capability-gap analysis
  • Go-to-market and channel strategy
  • Scenario modelling and prioritisation
  • Costed, phased execution roadmap
