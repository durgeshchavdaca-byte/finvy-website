ESOP Valuation Cost in India

What does an ESOP valuation (Rule 11UA / FMV / 409A) cost in India? Detailed breakdown of valuation methods, ranges, and how often you need to refresh — for startups and growth-stage companies.

Cost breakdown

What it costs.

All cost heads — government, regulatory, advisory and ancillary. Transparent, with typical ranges.

  • Rule 11UA Indian Income Tax valuation: ₹50,000 – ₹3,00,000 per valuation
  • 409A US valuation (for Delaware C-Corp): $1,500 – $5,000 per valuation
  • FMV valuation (FEMA, ICDR): ₹50,000 – ₹2,00,000
  • NAV-based valuation (asset-rich businesses): ₹30,000 – ₹1,00,000
  • DCF-based valuation (operating businesses): ₹1,00,000 – ₹5,00,000
  • Annual refresh (if cap-table unchanged): ₹50,000 – ₹1,50,000
  • Triggering event refresh (priced round, M&A): full valuation required
  • Valuation report turnaround: 1–3 weeks for standard; 4–6 weeks for complex multi-method
  • BQP ESOP valuation package (Rule 11UA + 409A combined): from ₹1,50,000
  • Bulk pricing available for portfolio companies of PE/VC funds
