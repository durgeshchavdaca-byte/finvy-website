FUNDRAISING STRATEGY

Capital Advisory

Securing the right capital at the right time is the single most important accelerant for growth-stage companies. Our Capital Advisory practice combines deep investor networks, transaction structuring expertise, and sector-specific intelligence to help founders close rounds faster, on better terms, and with the right partners at the table.

Process

How we work.

A structured engagement — from first conversation to delivered outcome. Each mandate follows a transparent process so founders always know what's next.

Step 01 / 05

Discovery

We assess your business model, unit economics, runway, and capital needs.

Step 02 / 05

Structuring

Design the deal architecture: instrument terms, cap table impact, valuation framework.

Step 03 / 05

Materials

Build investment-grade financial models, pitch decks, data rooms, and collateral packages.

Step 04 / 05

Matchmaking

Activate our curated network of domestic and international investors.

Step 05 / 05

Negotiation & Close

Lead term sheet negotiations, optimize SHA/SPA terms, and manage closing end-to-end.

Scope

What we cover.

The full range of expertise we bring to every engagement in this practice.

  • Equity fundraising (Seed to Series C+) + Debt structuring
  • Venture debt advisory - term loans, revenue-based financing, working capital lines
  • SAFE, convertible notes, and hybrid instrument design
  • Strategic investor matchmaking (domestic & international)
  • Cross-border capital structuring under FEMA, ODI, ECB frameworks
  • Financial modeling and investment-grade collateral preparation
  • Term sheet negotiation, SHA/SPA drafting, and investor alignment
  • Cap table restructuring, optimization, and dilution modeling
  • Valuation services - DCF, 409A, FMV, NAV-based approaches
