/ Core scope

What accounting outsourcing covers.

  • Daily / weekly transaction entry: AP invoices, AR invoices, expense categorisation, journal entries.
  • Bank + credit card reconciliation: daily or weekly, matching cleared transactions.
  • AR management: invoicing, aging reports, collection follow-up (if scope includes).
  • AP management: bill entry, approval workflow, payment scheduling, vendor reconciliation.
  • Payroll: integration with ADP / Gusto / Paylocity / Rippling + reconciliation to G/L.
  • Month-end close: accruals, prepaid amortisation, depreciation, inventory adjustments, bank rec sign-off. Standard 5-10 business day close.
  • Financial statements: P&L + Balance Sheet + Cash Flow + supporting schedules.
  • Year-end: close + audit preparation + schedules + sample support for external auditor.
  • Sales tax / VAT compliance: US state sales tax, UK VAT, EU VAT, Australian GST filing coordination.

/ Accounting standards supported

GAAP / IFRS / Ind AS.

  • US GAAP: ASC 606 (Revenue), ASC 842 (Leases), ASC 718 (Stock Compensation), ASC 326 (CECL), ASC 740 (Taxes).
  • IFRS: IFRS 15, 16, 9, 2, 12. Reconciliation to US GAAP where parent is US.
  • Ind AS: for Indian subsidiaries.
  • UK FRS 102 / FRS 105: for UK SMBs.
  • Australian AASB: for Australian SMBs.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Why outsource accounting to India?
Three main reasons: (1) Cost efficiency - India accounting labour is materially lower than US / UK / AU equivalents while quality (CA-led) is comparable or better. (2) Scale flexibility - add or reduce FTE support without hiring/firing locally. (3) Time zone advantage - India team works overnight in US time, delivering morning results. CPA firms and SMBs across US / UK / Australia routinely outsource SMB bookkeeping + close work to India.
Which accounting software does BQP work in?
QuickBooks Online + Desktop, Xero, Sage Intacct, NetSuite, Zoho Books, Tally for Indian clients. AP automation via Bill.com / Ramp / Melio / Stampli. Expense via Expensify / Dext / Divvy. Direct portal access with user-level permissions configured per client policy.
What's the typical monthly close timeline?
5-10 business day close for most SMBs. Day 1-3: transaction entry + bank/CC rec. Day 4-5: accruals + prepaid + depreciation + inventory. Day 6-7: review + adjustments. Day 8-10: financial statements + MIS pack + client review call. Faster close possible with proper documentation + clear accruals policy.
Can India team support US GAAP vs IFRS vs Ind AS?
Yes - BQP CAs have GAAP + IFRS specialisation. Reconciliation between standards (US GAAP to IFRS, Ind AS to IFRS, Ind AS to US GAAP) is standard output. ASC 606 (revenue), ASC 842 (leases), ASC 718 (SBC), ASC 326 (CECL), ASC 740 (taxes) are all handled under GAAP; IFRS 15 / 16 / 9 / 2 under IFRS.
Does outsourcing to India raise data security concerns?
A fair concern, addressed by: NDA + MSA with the India firm, DPDP Act 2023 compliance (India's data protection law), a SOC 2 report where the provider holds one (ask for it), encrypted file transfer (SFTP / cloud with 2FA), access logs, no local file downloads, time-bound access removal on engagement exit. BQP follows all standard data-security protocols for US / UK / EU clients.
