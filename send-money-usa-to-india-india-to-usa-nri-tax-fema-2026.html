/ Indians in US · Money to and from India · Updated 2026-11-06

Send Money From the USA to India (and India to the USA) in 2026: 1% Remittance Tax, Form 3520, FBAR and FEMA, without IRS or FEMA surprises.

From the US: pay from your US bank account or card and the 1% federal remittance tax (from 1 January 2026) does not apply; it hits transfers paid in cash, money order or cashier's cheque. Gifts above USD 19,000 to one person in 2026 need Form 709, though tax is rarely due. Money received by relatives in India is tax-free for them. From India: NRE balances move freely; NRO money (rent, dividends, property sale proceeds) up to USD 1 million a year with Form 15CA and a CA's Form 15CB. If you receive more than USD 100,000 in gifts from parents in India in a year, file Form 3520; Indian accounts above USD 10,000 in total need an FBAR.

/ US to India

Sending money home.

On the US side

  • 1% remittance tax (from 1 January 2026) on transfers funded with cash, money orders or cashier's cheques. Bank-account and US-card funded transfers are outside it.
  • Gift tax return: gifts above USD 19,000 per person in 2026 need Form 709; tax is rarely payable because of the lifetime exemption.

On the Indian side

  • Into your NRE account: only money earned or held abroad; interest is tax-free in India while you are non-resident and stays freely repatriable.
  • Into your NRO account: allowed; NRO interest is taxable in India (tax deducted at source, reducible to the treaty rate with a tax residency certificate and Form 10F).
  • To parents or other relatives: a gift from a relative is not taxable for them in India, whatever the amount. Keep the bank's inward remittance advice and a short gift note.
  • To friends: gifts from non-relatives are taxable for the receiver once the year's total exceeds INR 50,000, and then in full.

On the US side

  • Form 3520: if you receive more than USD 100,000 in a year as gifts from non-resident aliens (such as parents in India). It is a reporting form, not a tax, but late filing is penalised heavily.
  • FBAR and Form 8938: Indian bank accounts (NRE, NRO, FCNR) are foreign accounts. FBAR if the combined balance went above USD 10,000 at any time; Form 8938 above USD 50,000 at year-end or USD 75,000 at any time (single, living in the US).
  • Indian mutual funds are generally passive foreign investment companies (PFICs) for US persons, with punitive US tax: think before buying them.
  • NRE interest is tax-free in India but taxable in the US.

/ Indian income while abroad

Tax in both places, credit once.

  • Interest on NRO deposits, rent and capital gains in India are taxed in India first, usually by tax deducted at source.
  • With a tax residency certificate from your country and Form 10F, the bank can deduct at the treaty rate instead of the full rate, and you claim credit for Indian tax on your local return.
  • File an Indian return to claim refunds of excess TDS and to carry forward losses.
  • Moving back to India one day? Foreign accounts and pensions must be reported once you become resident and ordinarily resident. If you missed that, see FAST-DS for foreign pensions and accounts (declarations close 31 December 2026).

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is there a tax on sending money from the US to India?
Only the 1% federal remittance excise tax, from 1 January 2026, on transfers paid in cash, by money order or cashier's cheque. Transfers paid from a US bank account or US card are not covered. India does not tax the money received; gifts to relatives in India are tax-free for them.
Do I need Form 3520 for money from my parents in India?
Yes, if gifts from non-resident aliens total more than USD 100,000 in the year. It is information only, not a tax.
Do I report my NRE account to the IRS?
Yes. It counts towards the USD 10,000 FBAR threshold and Form 8938, and NRE interest is taxable on your US return even though it is tax-free in India.
How much money can I bring from India to the US?
NRE and FCNR balances: no limit. NRO balances and property sale proceeds: up to USD 1 million per financial year with Form 15CA and Form 15CB.
