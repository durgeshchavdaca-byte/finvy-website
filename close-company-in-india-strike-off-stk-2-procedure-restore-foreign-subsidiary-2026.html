How to Close a Company in India (2026): Strike Off in Form STK-2, Conditions, Restoring a Struck-Off Company and Closing a Foreign Subsidiary, clear the liabilities first, then file STK-2.

The simplest way to close a private company in India is voluntary strike off under section 248(2) of the Companies Act, 2013: once all liabilities are paid, the company files Form STK-2 with a special resolution (or consent of 75% of members by paid-up capital), indemnity and affidavits from directors, and a statement of accounts certified by a chartered accountant. The Registrar publishes a notice and then strikes the name off; applications are processed centrally by the MCA's C-PACE. A company that still has assets to distribute uses voluntary liquidation under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code instead. A struck-off company can be restored by the NCLT under section 252.

Strike off or liquidation.

Strike off by the Registrar (section 248(1))Company inactive and not filingNot a planned exit: directors can be disqualified and liabilities continue

The company cannot apply for strike off if, in the previous three months, it changed its name or registered office, disposed of property or rights for value, or carried on business other than what is needed to close (section 249).

/ Closing checklist

In this order.

  • Stop operations; settle vendors, employees (final salary, gratuity, PF and ESI exits) and statutory dues.
  • File all pending income tax, TDS and GST returns; cancel the GST registration; close professional tax and other registrations.
  • Prepare a statement of accounts showing nil assets and liabilities, certified by a chartered accountant (not older than 30 days at filing).
  • Pass the board and shareholder resolutions; directors sign the indemnity (STK-3) and affidavits (STK-4).
  • File Form STK-2 on the MCA portal with the fee; the Registrar publishes the notice (STK-6) and, after the objection period, strikes off the name.
  • Close the bank account after the final payments, and keep the records for the statutory period.

Foreign parent closing its Indian subsidiary?

The order matters: settle tax and GST, close payroll, clear inter-company balances, repatriate the remaining capital through the bank, and only then apply for strike off or liquidation. WhatsApp CA Durgesh Chavda.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How do I close a private limited company in India?
If it has no assets or liabilities, file for voluntary strike off in Form STK-2 under section 248(2) of the Companies Act, 2013, with a special resolution, director indemnity and affidavits, and a CA-certified statement of accounts. Companies with assets to distribute use voluntary liquidation under the IBC.
What does strike off mean for a company?
The company's name is removed from the register and it ceases to exist as a legal entity, but the liabilities of directors, officers and members continue.
Who processes strike-off applications?
The MCA's Centre for Processing Accelerated Corporate Exit (C-PACE), which handles voluntary strike-off filings centrally.
Can a struck-off company be revived?
Yes, by the NCLT under section 252: an appeal within 3 years of the Registrar's order, or an application within 20 years of gazette publication where the company was carrying on business or it is otherwise just.
How does a foreign company close its Indian subsidiary?
Settle all liabilities and taxes, close payroll and GST, repatriate remaining funds through the bank, then strike off (if nothing is left) or use voluntary liquidation.
