How to Get EIN as a Foreign Founder

Step-by-step guide to getting an EIN (Employer Identification Number) from the IRS without an SSN. Required for US bank account, Stripe, payroll, taxes.

Steps

The process.

How to get ein as a foreign founder.

Step 01 / 07

Determine entity type first

EIN application requires a US entity. Form your Delaware C-Corp or LLC first.

Step 02 / 07

Get an ITIN or designate responsible party

IRS requires an SSN/ITIN OR a 'responsible party' with one. As foreign founder, you can be the responsible party listing your foreign address.

Step 03 / 07

Complete Form SS-4

Download from IRS.gov. Fill in entity details, founder info, business reason. For non-SSN founders, write 'Foreign' where SSN is asked.

Step 04 / 07

Fax to IRS

International applicants must fax: +1 855-641-6935 (international fax). Online EIN tool requires SSN, so it's not available.

Step 05 / 07

Wait for EIN letter

Typical 1-4 weeks. IRS faxes back the EIN. Save the CP575 notice — it's your official EIN letter.

Step 06 / 07

Apply by phone (alternative)

Call IRS Business & Specialty Tax Line: +1 267-941-1099 (international). Faster but limited hours.

Step 07 / 07

Set up IRS online account

Once EIN received, create IRS account to file taxes online, request transcripts, etc.
