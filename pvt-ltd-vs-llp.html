Private Limited Company vs LLP

Choosing between a Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd) and a Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) shapes your funding, taxation, compliance and exit for years. Here is the CA-led comparison.

Side-by-side

Quick comparison.

Private Limited (Pvt Ltd) vs LLP for Indian startups and businesses.

CriterionPrivate Limited CompanyLLP
Best forVC-fundable startups, businesses planning equity issuanceService / professional firms, no equity-raising plans
Separate legal entityYesYes
Minimum members2 directors, 2 shareholders2 partners
Equity / fundraisingYes — preferred for VC fundingNo — only partner capital contribution
FDI / foreign investment100% FDI (most sectors) under automatic route100% FDI allowed but case-by-case for some sectors
ESOPsStandard — can issue stock optionsNot directly available; profit-sharing only
Tax rate22%–25% (corporate rate)30% (firm rate)
MAT / AMTMAT appliesAMT applies
Annual complianceAudit (mandatory > thresholds), ROC filings, statutory registersLighter — Form 8, Form 11; audit only above thresholds
Compliance cost (typical/year)₹40,000–₹2,00,000+₹15,000–₹50,000
ConversionCannot convert to LLP if FDI, ECB, equity holders existCan convert to Pvt Ltd via Companies Act process
